# Nita's public software collection

> A curated collection of software utilities that I wrote, mainly in LabVIEW and JS, for research, teaching or just for fun. Some of them have little utility, I use this site as a repository.

* **Author:** NitaD, Université Paris-Saclay
* **Last Updated:** October 21, 2026
* **Total Applications:** 112

---

## 1. Crystallography

### Symmetry
* [**Miller Planes**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/Miller.html): Visualize directions or crystallographic planes. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**Stereographic**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/stereo.html): Visualize point symmetry operations with stereographic projections. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**2D Space groups**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/2D_space_groups.html): Symmetry and positions in the 2D space groups. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**Point Group Symmetry**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/point_symmetry.html): Motifs and symmetry operations for the 32 point groups. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**3D Space Groups**](https://nitad54448.github.io/SpaceExplorer/): Tables of symmetry, positions and hkl rules for 527 settings of 3D space groups. (Language: JavaScript, Python) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/SpaceExplorer))

### Simulations
* [**Reciprocal Space Viewer**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/RSpace.html): Visualizes reciprocal lattice, distances, and Brillouin zones. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**PDF Explorer**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/pdf.html): Interactive Pair Distribution Function g(d) for 2D arrangements. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**2D patterns generator**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/patterns.html): Generates images based on the 17 space groups symmetry. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**3D Symmetry**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/symmetry_tool.html): Analyzes symmetry operations and translations in 3D. (Language: JavaScript)

## 2. Diffraction & Scattering

### Powder Diffraction
* [**Brutus**](https://nitad54448.github.io/brutus/): GPU-based powder diffraction indexing (exhaustive search). (Language: JavaScript, Python) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/brutus))
* [**Powder 5**](https://nitad54448.github.io/powder5/powder5.html): Unit cell refinements with LeBail method or Pawley extraction for X-ray/neutron powder diffraction patterns. Charge flipping or Monte-Carlo structure search with WebGPU. (Language: JavaScript, Python) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/powder5))
* [**compHarker**](https://nitad54448.github.io/compHarker/): Composition driven analysis of Patterson/Harker maps with Metropolis algorithm on WebGPU. (Language: JavaScript, Python) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/compHarker))
* [**Harko**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Harko/Harko.html): Analyze Harker planes in Patterson maps with Swarm technology. (Language: JavaScript, Python) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/Harko))
* [**IndexMan**](https://nitad54448.github.io/indexMan/IndexMan.html): Generate HKL for any space group; manually index patterns. (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/indexMan))
* [**Combs**](https://nitad54448.github.io/combs/combs.html): Powder diffraction lattice indexing (combinatorial hkl search). (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/combs))
* [**powder4_source**](https://github.com/nitad54448/powder4_source): Legacy utility for converting, indexing, simulating, and analyzing powder data. (Language: VB / Fortran)
* [**Misfit-fit**](https://nitad54448.github.io/misfit/misfit.html): Lattice parameter fit (LeBail) for modulated structures. (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/misfit))
* [**Simple Cubic Powder XRD**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/scpxrd.html): Simulate the simple cubic powder X-ray diffraction. (Language: JavaScript)

### Methods
* [**Ewald Sphere Viewer**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/ewald.html): Visualizes the Ewald sphere construction in reciprocal space. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**Single Crystal Diffraction**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/4-circles.html): Advanced simulator for 4-circle experiments. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**2D diffraction**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/2D_diffraction.html): Diffraction patterns of 2D structures. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**Powder Diffraction**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/Powder.html): Visualizes polycrystalline diffraction. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**Laue Diffraction**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Laue/Laue.html): Simulator for single-crystal Laue diffraction. (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/Laue))
* [**Orient-a-Laue**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Laue/Orient.html): Simulate Laue patterns and orient single crystals (experimental). (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/Laue))
* [**Patterson function**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Patterson/Patterson.html): Demonstrates structure, diffraction pattern, and Patterson function. (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/Patterson))

### Reflectivity & Wave Dynamics
* [**WaveXplorer**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/WaveXplorer.html): Visualizing fundamental concepts of waves. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**Kiessig Fringes**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/kiessig.html): X-ray Reflectivity for a single film on a substrate. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**X-ray reflectivity**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/xrr.html): Multi-layers X-ray Reflectivity (XRR) calculator. (Language: JavaScript)

## 3. Physical Properties & Transport

### Thermal
* [**Seebeck Calculator**](https://github.com/nitad54448/calculate_Seebeck): Calculates absolute Seebeck coefficient for thermoelectric legs. (Language: LabVIEW)
* [**Online Seebeck Calculator**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/seebeck.html): Calculates thermoelectric power for common thermocouples. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**3-omega 2D fit**](https://nitad54448.github.io/calc_3omega/): Calculates thermal properties from 3-omega experiments (2D model). (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/calc_3omega))
* [**3-omega Linear fit**](https://nitad54448.github.io/calc_3omega/3omega_linear_fit.html): Calculates thermal conductivity via linear fitting of 3rd harmonic. (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/calc_3omega))
* [**Simulate 3-omega**](https://nitad54448.github.io/sim_3omega/): Simulates harmonic signals for thermal conductivity experiments. (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/sim_3omega))
* [**Versatile**](https://github.com/nitad54448/Versatile): Legacy program for calculating non-isothermal kinetic parameters. Written in Visual Basic in 1994-1995, upgrade of an older program written in Quick Basic in 1988. (Language: Visual Basic)

### Electrical & Magnetic
* [**HT-Halbach**](https://github.com/nitad54448/Halbach): System for measuring Hall effect at high temperature (DC and AC). (Language: LabVIEW)
* [**Calculate Hall signal**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Hall/): Calculates Hall signals for various conditions. (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/Hall))
* [**Halbach array simulator**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Halbach_simulator/Halbach.html): Simulates the magnetic field in some Halbach arrays built with discrete cylindrical components. (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/Halbach_simulator))
* [**Mandhala**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Halbach_simulator/Mandhala.html): Exploration of the magnetic field (equivalent to k=2 in a Halbach setup) obtained with exotic distributions of dipoles (fullerene, Lebedev and Fibonacci). (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/Halbach_simulator))
* [**Van der Pauw Calculator**](https://github.com/nitad54448/van_der_Pauw_Labview): Calculates resistivity from van der Pauw method measurements. (Language: LabVIEW)
* [**Programmable Resistor**](https://github.com/nitad54448/programmable_Resistor): Design and simulate a programmable resistor for automated testing. (Language: LabVIEW)

### Diffusion
* [**Fick's Laws**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Fick/diffusion_main.html): Simulation of 1D diffusion processes and concentration profiles. (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/Fick))
* [**Onsager's diffusion model**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Fick/onsager.html): Simulation of 1D multi-component diffusion processes, fluxes and concentration profiles with the Onsager's model. (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/Fick))
* [**ThermoMigrate**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Fick/ThermoMigrate.html): Explore thermal migration in thermocouple legs. (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/Fick))

## 4. Spectroscopy & Imaging

### Spectroscopy

* [**Sauerbrey**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Sauerbrey/sauerbrey.html): Estimate deposition rates with QCM. (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/Sauerbrey))
* [**Gap**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Tauc/gap.html): Estimate bandgap with simple linearisation of Tauc plots. (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/Tauc))
* [**X-ray Emission Spectra**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/Emission.html): Generates and displays X-ray emission spectra. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**X-ray Absorption & Filters**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/filtres.html): Simulates the effect of a filter on an X-ray spectrum. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**X-ray Monochromators**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/monochromateur.html): Effect of a single crystal monochromator on an X-ray spectrum. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**EXAFS simulations**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/exafs.html): Simple EXAFS simulations for single paths on 2D structures. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**Atomic Energy Levels**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/niveaux_atomiques.html): Schematically displays atomic energy levels (K, L, M). (Language: JavaScript)
* [**High Pressure Calculator**](https://github.com/nitad54448/high_pressure_Ruby): Calculates pressure from ruby fluorescence line shift. (Language: LabVIEW)

### Imaging & Transforms
* [**2D Radon Transform**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/Radon.html): Computes and displays the 2D Radon Transform (Sinogram). (Language: JavaScript)
* [**Color Radon Transform**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/ColorRadon.html): Explorer for the 2D Radon Transform on color images. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**3D Radon Transform**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/3D_Radon.html): A 3D explorer for computed tomography. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**2D Hough Transform**](https://nitad54448.github.io/Enseignement/Hough.html): An interactive explorer for line detection in images. (Language: JavaScript)
* [**Simple camera**](https://nitad54448.github.io/apod/camera.html): Camera feed with flip, markers and scale. (Language: JavaScript) ([Repository](https://github.com/nitad54448/apod))
